Only #1's claimed advanced by 323 and its claimable became zero; the other 99 entitlements remained preserved. **B actually received 323 raw, equal to #1's 323 raw accumulated but unclaimed before transfer.** Receipt attribution uses raw events and the Vault ledger, rather than substituting unrelated wallet balance changes.
