## 5. Failure and Refund Paths

`contribute` and its internal purchase are one atomic transaction. If the market call or postconditions fail when B completes funding, the entire transaction reverts: B's principal is not transferred into the Vault, B receives no new shares, and no purchase occurs. Gas is still consumed; creator's earlier 0.20301 BNB/67 shares remain. **A revert neither automatically switches the pool to REFUNDABLE nor automatically refunds creator.**

Before a purchase, holders may call `withdraw(theirOwnIDs, theirOwnAddress)` directly in either FUNDING or REFUNDABLE. Call `markRefundable()` separately once an allowed terminal reason is established; the contract accepts deadline expiration and CANCELLED, COUNTER_CHANGED, FILLED, EXPIRED, LISTING_ENDED, PRICE_CHANGED. OWNER_CHANGED, APPROVAL_MISSING, or UNKNOWN alone are not permitted early-termination reasons. If necessary, the designated guardian can make a FUNDING pool refundable, but still cannot withdraw funds or alter holders' beneficial rights.

A refund also needs a separate plan and separate human confirmation. Its receipt must match `Withdrawn` participant/recipient/all IDs/exact principal, all `IdReleased` events, ERC-1155 burns, and balances reduced to zero. fundedWei/soldCount must decrease and totalPrincipalRefunded must increase accordingly. If only creator contributed, the refund should be **0.20301 BNB**; B has no refundable balance. recipient is bound to that holder's own address. A failed refund call reverts the whole transaction and cannot be marked “refunded.” ACTIVE pools no longer permit these principal exits; post-purchase redemption must not be confused with a refund.
